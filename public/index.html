Assumptions

  • Distances use an Earth radius of 6,371 km and a Moon radius of 1,737.4 km. Ground-to-ground modes subtract both.
  • A LEGO stud nests into the tube below it, so stacking flat adds nothing. Stack the same brick on its side and the stud has nowhere to go, which is why the count jumps.
  • Masonry courses include a mortar joint on whichever face you are bedding, so it applies in every orientation.
  • Prices are rough single-unit figures and are there for scale, not for budgeting.
  • The tower is treated as a straight line. Gravity, structural collapse and orbital mechanics are somebody else's problem.
